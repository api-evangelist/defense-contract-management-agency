# Defense Contract Management Agency (DCMA)

> The Defense Contract Management Agency is the Department of Defense component that works
> directly with defense suppliers to help ensure that DoD, federal, and allied government
> supplies and services are delivered on time, at projected cost, and meet performance
> requirements. DCMA performs contract administration, quality assurance, and earned value
> management oversight.

## API surface

DCMA publishes **no public developer API**. There is no developer portal, no OpenAPI or other
machine-readable contract, no SDK, no MCP server and no agent card on any host this record
knows. DCMA's contract administration eTools — the contract administration services directory,
shared data warehouse single sign-on, commercial item determination, audit tracking and action
tool, delivery schedule manager and final contract closeout — have been migrating into the
Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment (PIEE, https://piee.eb.mil), a Department of
Defense platform reached with a government credential. PIEE is operated by DoD, not by DCMA,
so nothing served there is attributed to this record.
